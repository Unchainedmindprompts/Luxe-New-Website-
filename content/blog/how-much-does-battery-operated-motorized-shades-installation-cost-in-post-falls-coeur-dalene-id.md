## How to compare motorized shade prices for your windows

A single “price per window” leaves out too much. A small bedroom window and a wide living-room window can need different fabric sizes, hardware, and motors. Comparing the same dimensions and fabric with two operating options gives you a much better sense of what you are paying for.

1. Enter the approximate width and height of each window in the [instant estimator](/estimate).
2. Choose a shade category and the light-filtering or room-darkening option that fits the room.
3. Compare cordless and motorized operation for the same windows.
4. Review the summary, including the remote controls, before comparing the project total.

You do not need exact ordering measurements to begin, and no email address is required. We take the final measurements before placing your custom order. If your size or configuration is outside the calculator’s options, bring it to the consultation rather than forcing it into a size that doesn’t fit.

### Roller shades or cellular shades?

[Roller shades](/products/roller-shades) offer a simple fabric panel and a clean finish. Fabric, dimensions, and the cassette all contribute to the price. The estimator’s roller options include a round cassette and a choice of cordless or rechargeable motorized operation.

[Cellular shades](/products/cellular-shades) use a honeycomb construction and offer a different look and insulating structure. The estimator covers select ¾-inch smooth cellular fabrics, with available top-down/bottom-up and motorized options. Compare the actual selections and prices for your windows rather than assuming one category is always less expensive.

## What is included in a Luxe motorized shade estimate?

### Remote controls and multiple shades

You don’t necessarily need a separate remote for every shade. The estimator groups compatible systems and shows the included remotes; extra remotes are optional. Different product categories can use different systems, so don’t assume one remote will control every product in the house.

This is also why multiplying a single-window example by ten may overstate a whole-room estimate. Enter the windows together so the calculator can account for the compatible remote groups.

### App control, voice control, and additional equipment

A rechargeable motor and remote do not automatically include app control or a connection to your voice assistant. Those features depend on the selected system and may need a hub, gateway, or other equipment. We confirm what works with your home and quote any additional equipment separately.

For high windows, we also discuss how you will reach the battery to charge it. A shade that is easy to operate should be practical to maintain, too.

## What changes the total project price?

### Window dimensions, fabric, and light control

Larger windows use more material and can have different hardware requirements. Fabric collections, liners, and light-control choices also affect price. Start with the rooms where privacy, glare, or daily convenience matters most, then compare options for the rest of the house.

### Motor and power options

Battery-powered shades are worth considering in an existing home because they usually avoid new wiring. Charging frequency varies with the motor, battery, shade size, and use; there is no single battery-life promise that fits every system.

For new construction, discuss hardwired options early enough for your electrician to plan the power supply. Electrical work is a separate consideration from the shade estimate. We can help identify the shade requirements before the wiring decisions are made.

### Installation access and unusual windows

Tall windows over stairs, specialty shapes, and unusual mounting surfaces deserve an in-person review. An online calculator cannot assess safe access or every mounting detail. We confirm the installation requirements and any applicable charges before you commit to the final quote.
