## What actually changes the price?

A useful quote is specific to your windows and your choices. These are the details that make the biggest difference:

- **Window size and layout.** A small bedroom window and a wide living-room opening are different projects. Mounting depth, access, and the number of treatments matter too.
- **Material and fabric.** The fabric, finish, opacity, and construction you select affect the price. Compare samples in your own light rather than choosing from a tiny screen image.
- **How the treatment operates.** Manual and motorized systems have different costs. Motorization may be especially useful on hard-to-reach windows; it does not have to go on every window.
- **Light and privacy needs.** Filtering daylight, reducing glare, and darkening a bedroom call for different choices. Fabric alone does not determine the finished result; mounting and edge gaps also matter.
- **Measuring and installation.** Check whether a quote includes these services and what support is available afterward.

That is why a single “price per window” can be misleading without knowing the window, product, and installation. We would rather look at your project and explain the options than give you a low starting number that doesn't describe what you need.

## Where can you save without regretting the choice?

### Start with the rooms that matter most

If covering the whole house stretches your budget, start with privacy in bedrooms and bathrooms, then tackle the remaining rooms as your budget allows. A single-window project is welcome too.

For a move-in, tell us your timing early. **When you order with Luxe, we provide and install temporary shades at no charge in priority rooms while you wait for your custom treatments.** We'll coordinate the timing with you. ### Compare simpler options before adding upgrades

[Custom blinds](/products/blinds), [roller shades](/products/roller-shades), and [cellular shades](/products/cellular-shades) are useful starting points to compare. Which offers the best value depends on your window size, selected materials, and how you want the room to work.

Ask to see what changes when you choose a different fabric or operating system. You may prefer the simpler option once you see it in your home.

### Put upgrades where you will actually use them

A motor on a high window you cannot comfortably reach may solve a daily frustration. The same upgrade on an easy-to-reach guest-room window may not be a priority. Spend on a benefit you care about, not a feature you feel you are supposed to have.

### Coordinate the look without buying the same treatment everywhere

Your bedroom and kitchen do not have to use the same product. We can help you compare colors and finishes across rooms while choosing treatments for each room's privacy, light, and everyday use.

## How to compare two quotes fairly

Before choosing by the total—or the advertised discount—check that you're comparing the same scope:

- Which windows and products are included?
- What fabric, material, opacity, and operating system are specified?
- Are measuring, installation, and any necessary hardware included?
- What is the estimated lead time?
- What does the product warranty cover, and who handles installation-related issues?

If you already have another quote, bring it to the consultation. We can explain differences in product and scope so you can make an informed decision. A bigger percentage discount alone doesn't tell you which quote is the better fit.
