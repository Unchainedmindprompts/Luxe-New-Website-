## Familiar Controls Throughout Your Home

<p>You might want roller shades in your living room, top-down/bottom-up cellular shades in your bedroom, and shades with adjustable vanes in your dining room. Each serves a different purpose, but operating them should feel familiar.</p>

<p>With PowerView Gen 3, the same remote can raise and lower standard shades, adjust the middle rail on compatible top-down/bottom-up shades, and control vanes on products such as Silhouette and Pirouette shadings. You can select a shade or a group, depending on how we configure your system.</p>

<p>That means you can choose the right window treatment for each room while keeping a consistent way to operate everything. And when you don’t feel like reaching for your phone, you don’t have to.</p>

## Apple Home Convenience, With Security Built In

<p>Security is another reason I value the Apple ecosystem, and PowerView fits into that experience through a manufacturer-supported Apple Home connection.</p>

<p>PowerView Gen 3 connects to Apple Home through Matter using a PowerView Gateway. After setup, you can operate your shades in the Home app or ask Siri to adjust them—without an unofficial third-party bridge or software workaround.</p>

<p>Apple Home’s Matter connections use encrypted communication and device authentication to help protect access to your connected accessories. For me, the appeal is having a supported connection within the system I already use and trust.</p>

<p>The PowerView app is used for setup, and the Gateway remains part of the system. Once connected, everyday shade control can happen right in Apple Home, alongside your other compatible devices.</p>

## Your Favorite Positions, Ready When You Want Them

<p>Think about how you use your shades throughout the day. You might lower the top slightly for morning light, adjust them to reduce afternoon glare, and close them for privacy in the evening.</p>

<p>PowerView Gen 3 lets you save two favorite positions for each shade and recall them through the remote or app. Multiple selected shades can each move to their own saved position.</p>

<p>That’s especially useful with top-down/bottom-up shades. Once you find the balance of daylight and privacy you like, you can save it instead of adjusting everything by eye each time.</p>



## Shades That Fit Your Daily Routine

<p>With the appropriate PowerView setup, you can schedule your shades to move throughout the day. Open them in the morning, adjust them when the afternoon sun comes around, and close them for evening privacy. Away-from-home control is also available with a connected PowerView Gateway.</p>

<p>If you use Apple Home, you can create scenes that include your shades and other compatible accessories. A “Good Night” scene, for example, could close your shades and turn off selected lights. Apple Home scenes are configured separately from your PowerView scenes.</p>

<p>These are the conveniences you start to appreciate once you’re living with the system—especially when you have several windows to manage.</p>
